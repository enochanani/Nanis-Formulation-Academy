Why Nanis

Formulation, not decoration

Most short courses teach a recipe. Nanis Formulations Academy teaches the science underneath the recipe, so a graduate can troubleshoot a failed batch, adjust a formula when a raw material changes, and scale a product from a beaker to a production line with confidence.

Every certificate programme sits inside a wider system built around five schools, a three level pathway, and a research centre dedicated to Ghanaian and African raw materials.

Led by a working formulator. The academy is founded by Enoch Anani; the Lead Formulator of Nanis Formulations, a manufacturing firm, which means every module is grounded in what actually happens on a production floor.

The Nanis Learning Pathway

Three levels, one direction: from principle to practice

Every student follows the same structure, whichever school they eventually specialise in. The concentration rises as the training progresses, from scientific thinking to a finished, market ready formulation business.

Before you enroll

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions we get on WhatsApp, answered here first.

Q01Is training online or in person?+
Nanis Formulations Academy uses a blended approach. Foundations and theory are delivered online, while practicals take place on site at the formulation centre. For practicals, students may choose to work from their own space or attend in person at the centre.
Q02Do I need my own equipment or ingredients?+
No equipment is required before you start. A full prospectus listing the tools and ingredients you will need is shared during training. Weekly assignment materials, ingredients and tools are provided at the formulation centre, and you can choose to have these delivered to you or come in and complete your assignment on site.
Q03How big are the classes?+
Class sizes are kept small so every student gets close attention. Beginner classes take 5 to 10 students, Intermediate classes take 10 to 15, and Advanced Formulator classes take 3 to 5.
Q04Is there an age requirement?+
There is no strict age requirement. All safety protocols are strictly observed throughout training regardless of age.
Q05Do I get a certificate at the end?+
Yes. A certificate is issued at a graduation ceremony on completion of your programme.
Q06How much does training cost?+
Fees are shared directly on request. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send the current fee schedule for your chosen track.
Q07Can I pay in instalments?+
Yes, instalment plans are available. Ask us on WhatsApp for the current payment options.
Q08Are fees refundable?+
No. Fees are non-refundable.
